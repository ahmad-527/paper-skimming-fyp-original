RESEARCH & PROVENANCE

From research
to a reading tool.

This undergraduate project compares sentence classifiers for biomedical RCT abstracts. The live interface uses the existing December 2024 tribrid checkpoint: Universal Sentence Encoder, character BiLSTM and sentence-position features.

The papers

  • Dernoncourt & Lee (2017), PubMed 200k RCT ↗
  • Dernoncourt, Lee & Szolovits, Neural Networks for Joint Sentence Classification in Medical Paper Abstracts ↗

The project supplies sentence position as features; it does not implement joint sequence decoding.
